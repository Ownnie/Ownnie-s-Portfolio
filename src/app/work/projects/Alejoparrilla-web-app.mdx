## Overview
AlejoParrilla Webapp is a subscription and discount voucher management platform designed to streamline restaurant operations. This solution allows users to manage their subscriptions and discount campaigns on a centralized system, reducing administrative overhead and enhancing customer retention.

## Key Features
- **Automated Subscription Handling**: Users can subscribe and receive discounts automatically without manual intervention.
- **Real-Time Voucher Management**: Restaurants can create, track, and distribute vouchers instantly.
- **User Authentication & Security**: Secure login and access control implemented with Firebase.

## Technologies Used
- **React.js**: Frontend framework for a dynamic and responsive UI.
- **Firebase**: Backend and database services for authentication and data storage.
- **Tailwind CSS**: For modern and scalable styling.

## Challenges and Learnings
- **Scalability**: Optimizing Firebase queries to handle large amounts of user data efficiently.
- **User Engagement**: Ensuring smooth user experience and reducing friction in voucher redemption.

## Outcome
- Reduced manual workload for restaurant staff by **100%**.
- Increased customer engagement and retention through personalized offers.
- Improved operational efficiency by centralizing all subscription and discount management.
